# Mid-Atlantic to Montréal

Route: Maryland / Delaware / New Jersey → New York → Montréal, Québec.

Ephemera and activities for this trip live in the theme folders below.

- `bingo/` — billboard/sign bingo boards
- `word-search/` — printable word search puzzles
- `coloring/` — printable coloring pages
- `maze/` — printable mazes
- `checklists/` — packing/prep/stop checklists
- `maps/` — route maps, stop lists
- `language/` — phrasebooks, language-learning aids (useful once crossing
  into Québec)

## Materials

- [`bingo/billboard-bingo-md-nj-de-ny-montreal/`](bingo/billboard-bingo-md-nj-de-ny-montreal/)
  — billboard bingo board covering the whole route.
- [`word-search/state-word-search-md-de-nj-ny-qc/`](word-search/state-word-search-md-de-nj-ny-qc/)
  — one word search per stop (MD, DE, NJ, NY, Montréal/Québec), with answer keys.
- [`coloring/five-states-map-coloring-page/`](coloring/five-states-map-coloring-page/)
  — a single coloring page collaging all five region outlines along the route.
- [`maze/state-shaped-mazes-md-de-nj-ny-qc/`](maze/state-shaped-mazes-md-de-nj-ny-qc/)
  — a maze carved into the shape of each region, in three difficulty tiers
  (Easy/Medium/Hard), with solutions.
- [`maze/montreal-borough-maze/`](maze/montreal-borough-maze/) — a maze
  carved into the real boroughs of the City of Montréal, with real
  landmarks marked, in three difficulty tiers (Easy/Medium/Hard).
